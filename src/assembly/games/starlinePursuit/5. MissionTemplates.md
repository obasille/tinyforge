## Mission Templates (Structure for Variety and Replay)

Mission templates define the overall scenario framing the chase. They change:

- target objective
- map emphasis
- player constraints
- win/loss conditions
- pacing

This is the main way to get high replayability without adding new mechanics.

Below is a complete catalog of mission types that fit the existing systems: sensors, trails, interception, resource pressure.

---

# 1. Standard Interception (Baseline)

### Premise

A high-value target is fleeing through the sector.

### Win

Interceptor enters the same system as the target.

### Lose

Target escapes via any exit node.

### Notes

Tutorial-friendly. Pure chase.

---

# 2. Timed Escape Window

### Premise

The target can only escape when a hyperspace gate opens.

### Win

Intercept before gate activation.

### Lose

Target survives until Turn N, then escapes automatically.

### Why it works

Prevents endpoint camping too early. Forces timing.

---

# 3. Rendezvous Mission (Meeting Point)

### Premise

The target is not escaping, but trying to meet an ally.

### Target Goal

Reach one of several rendezvous hubs between turns 10–15.

### Win

Intercept before meeting.

### Lose

Target arrives during the window → mission fails.

### Gameplay Impact

Player must predict not just location, but timing.

---

# 4. Smuggling Route Disruption

### Premise

Target is smuggling cargo through trade lanes.

### Special Rule

Target prefers hubs and trade relays strongly.

### Win

Intercept OR force target into a blockade trap.

### Lose

Target completes 3 successful “smuggling stops.”

### Gameplay Impact

Target loiters, creating trail opportunities.

---

# 5. Multi-Target Confusion (Decoys)

### Premise

Several signals appear, only one is real.

### Setup

2–3 moving targets on the map:

- one real
- others decoys

### Win

Intercept the real target.

### Lose

Too many turns wasted chasing decoys → real escapes.

### Gameplay Impact

Encourages triangulation and confirmation tools.

---

# 6. The Ghost Hunt (Stealth Target)

### Premise

An alien probe moves through nebula interference.

### Special Rules

- trails decay faster in nebula zones
- scans are weaker

### Win

Intercept before probe reaches nebula core.

### Lose

Probe survives N turns or reaches core system.

### Gameplay Impact

Focus shifts to persistent beacons over active scans.

---

# 7. Blockade and Capture (Containment)

### Premise

The target cannot be caught directly unless cornered.

### Special Rule

Interceptor can only capture if target has no valid escape neighbor.

### Win

Trap target in a dead end or surrounded hub.

### Lose

Target breaks containment and escapes.

### Gameplay Impact

Turns chase into a net-closing puzzle.

---

# 8. High-Speed Courier Sprint

### Premise

A diplomatic courier is extremely fast.

### Special Rule

Target occasionally moves 2 jumps/turn.

### Win

Intercept early, before it reaches outer rim.

### Lose

Courier escapes quickly.

### Gameplay Impact

Player must cut off, not trail-follow.

---

# 9. Hostage Convoy Escort (Reverse Chase)

### Premise

Player must protect a slow allied ship while hunting the target.

### Setup

- Allied convoy moves toward safe zone
- Target tries to intercept convoy

### Win

Intercept target before it reaches convoy.

### Lose

Target reaches convoy system.

### Gameplay Impact

Player must split attention: defense + pursuit.

---

# 10. Signal Extraction Mission

### Premise

The objective is not capture, but data.

### Win Condition

Keep target within beacon range for X total turns.

### Lose

Target escapes before enough signal is gathered.

### Gameplay Impact

Rewards surveillance network over direct chase.

---

# 11. Sector Sweep Operation (Limited Interceptor)

### Premise

Interceptor arrives late.

### Setup

First 8 turns: only scouts and sensors.
Turn 9: interceptor warps in at HQ.

### Win

Intercept after arrival.

### Lose

Target escapes before interceptor arrives.

### Gameplay Impact

Early game becomes pure tracking and prediction.

---

# 12. Attrition Mission (Resource Starvation)

### Premise

Target is evading until your fleet runs out of energy.

### Special Rule

Sensor energy regen reduced.
Hardware kits limited to 2.

### Win

Intercept despite scarcity.

### Lose

If player cannot issue scan actions for 3 turns → mission fails.

### Gameplay Impact

Every scan decision matters.

---

# 13. Cat-and-Mouse Duel (Intelligent Target)

### Premise

Target actively reacts to player interceptor position.

### Special Rule

Target has Tier 3 awareness:

- avoids traps
- reroutes dynamically

### Win

Outthink it with layered sensors.

### Lose

Survive until escape clock.

### Gameplay Impact

Most strategic version of the chase.

---

# 14. Final Net (Boss Mission)

### Premise

A decoy master is escaping with stolen tech.

### Special Rules

- drops false trails
- triggers fake beacon alerts occasionally
- has one stealth cooldown

### Win

Capture within 25 turns.

### Lose

Boss escapes or exhausts player resources.

### Gameplay Impact

Requires multi-confirmation and careful commitment.

---

# 15. Campaign Structure (Meta Variety)

A campaign can sequence mission templates:

1. Standard Interception (tutorial)
2. Smuggling Disruption
3. Ghost Hunt
4. Courier Sprint
5. Rendezvous Mission
6. Decoy Boss Finale

Unlocks:

- new ship types
- extra deployment kits
- improved trail detection

---

# Map Variants Per Mission

Mission templates also change map topology:

- Courier missions: long corridor maps
- Ghost missions: nebula-heavy clusters
- Boss missions: many hubs and loops
- Containment missions: more dead ends

This increases variety without adding mechanics.

---

# Summary: Why Mission Templates Matter

They ensure the same core systems produce many different games:

- sometimes you race
- sometimes you trap
- sometimes you surveil
- sometimes you identify the real target
- sometimes you defend something else

This keeps the chase loop fresh while remaining TinyForge-scale.
