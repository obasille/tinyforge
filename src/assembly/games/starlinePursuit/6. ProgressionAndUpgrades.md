## Progression and Upgrades (Campaign Growth Without Power Creep)

Progression should add variety and new decisions, not simply make the player stronger. The goal is to unlock **new tactical options** while keeping missions challenging through smarter targets, tighter clocks, and new map pressures.

This section defines a complete upgrade and progression structure for **Starline Pursuit**.

---

# 1. Progression Philosophy

Upgrades should:

- expand strategic choices
- introduce new tools and tradeoffs
- avoid trivializing interception
- encourage different playstyles

Avoid upgrades that are pure +speed or +range with no downside.

---

# 2. Campaign Progression Structure

A campaign is a sequence of missions with escalating complexity:

### Tier 1: Learning the Hunt

- Standard interception
- Smuggler disruption

Unlocks basic sensor tools.

### Tier 2: Uncertainty and Speed

- Courier sprint
- Rendezvous mission

Unlocks mobility and prediction tools.

### Tier 3: Stealth and Deception

- Ghost probe
- Multi-target decoys

Unlocks counter-stealth.

### Tier 4: Mastery

- Intelligent target
- Boss decoy master

Unlocks advanced containment.

---

# 3. Persistent Player Growth Resources

Between missions, the player earns:

### Tech Points (TP)

Used to unlock upgrades.

- Typical gain: 1–3 TP per mission
- Bonus TP for fast interception or low resource use

### Fleet Slots (FS)

Limits how many ships you can bring.

- Start: 3 ships
- Late campaign: 5 ships max

This prevents overwhelming coverage.

---

# 4. Ship Upgrade Trees

Each ship has 2–3 upgrade branches, forcing specialization.

---

## 4.1 Interceptor Frigate Upgrades

Role: capture ship, not a scanner.

### Branch A: Pursuit

- Afterburn Burst: 1 extra jump, costs extra fuel
- Faster cooldown on sprint

Tradeoff: burns fuel quickly

### Branch B: Containment

- Gravity Snare: target cannot leave system next turn (once per mission)
- Slower movement while snare is armed

Tradeoff: tactical trap vs speed

### Branch C: Boarding Precision

- Capture requires only adjacency (late upgrade)
- Higher CP cost to engage

Tradeoff: easier capture, more command pressure

---

## 4.2 Scout Corvette Upgrades

Role: trail confirmation and rapid investigation.

### Branch A: Trail Hunter

- Trails persist +1 turn when discovered
- Directional trail clue chance increased

Tradeoff: weaker movement while tracking mode active

### Branch B: Rapid Recon

- Investigate without stopping (instant but costs SE)
- Higher energy consumption

Tradeoff: speed vs resource drain

### Branch C: Shadowing

- If scout enters target system, target is revealed next turn even if it leaves

Tradeoff: scout becomes vulnerable/committed

---

## 4.3 Survey Cruiser Upgrades

Role: strategic intelligence.

### Branch A: Efficient Scanning

- Wide sweep costs less SE
- Faster cooldown

Tradeoff: reduced sweep radius

### Branch B: Precision Pulses

- Range pulses give stronger constraints (exact distance)

Tradeoff: higher CP cost

### Branch C: Counter-Stealth

- Nebula scans partially ignore interference

Tradeoff: long cooldown

---

## 4.4 Beacon Tender Upgrades

Role: long-term net building.

### Branch A: Expanded Coverage

- Beacon radius +1
- Boost costs less SE

Tradeoff: fewer total beacons carried

### Branch B: Rapid Deployment

- Deploy beacon instantly (no setup turn)

Tradeoff: beacon lifetime limited (expires after 6 turns)

### Branch C: Smart Triggers

- Beacon reports direction of departure

Tradeoff: higher SE upkeep

---

## 4.5 Drone Carrier Upgrades

Role: mobile surveillance.

### Branch A: Endurance Patrol

- Drone duration +2 turns

Tradeoff: slower redeployment

### Branch B: Wide Net

- Patrol radius increased

Tradeoff: weaker detection certainty (more ambiguous alerts)

### Branch C: Active Tagging

- If drone detects target, target leaves stronger trails for 2 turns

Tradeoff: expensive SE cost

---

## 4.6 Blockade Cruiser Upgrades (Late)

Role: endgame trapping.

### Branch A: Stronger Blockade

- Block lasts +1 turn

Tradeoff: immobilizes cruiser completely

### Branch B: Flexible Denial

- Can block from adjacent system

Tradeoff: shorter duration

### Branch C: Forced Reroute

- Target entering blocked lane leaves guaranteed fresh trail

Tradeoff: long cooldown

---

# 5. Hardware Unlock Progression

Deployment Kits remain scarce, but new options appear.

### Early Hardware

- Listening Beacon
- Sensor Relay

### Mid Hardware

- Decoy Buoy (herding)
- Burst Scanner Node (one-time deep scan)

### Late Hardware

- Trap Beacon (reveals target for 2 turns)
- Signal Jammer (reduces false trails)

Hardware upgrades are about **new tactical patterns**, not raw power.

---

# 6. Progression Through New Mechanics (Not Stats)

Instead of +10% speed, unlock:

- directional trail interpretation
- escape window prediction
- temporary lane denial
- target classification tools

These keep gameplay fresh.

---

# 7. Difficulty Scaling Alongside Progression

As the player gains tools, missions scale by:

- smarter target awareness tiers
- shorter escape clocks
- more nebula interference
- additional exits
- decoy targets
- limited starting intel

So the game stays challenging without feeling unfair.

---

# 8. Mission Rewards and Player Choice

After each mission:

- choose 1 upgrade OR +1 deployment kit OR +1 sensor capacity
- optional secondary objectives grant extra TP:
  - intercept within 15 turns
  - deploy ≤2 beacons
  - never let target enter outer rim

This creates build diversity.

---

# 9. Example Player Builds

### Net Controller

- beacon radius upgrades
- blockade denial
- slow but inevitable traps

### High-Speed Hunter

- interceptor burst
- scout rapid recon
- wins by early cutoff

### Intelligence Specialist

- survey efficiency
- counter-stealth
- dominates ghost/decoy missions

Replayability comes from different approaches.

---

# 10. Endgame Balance Rule

Even fully upgraded, the player should never cover everything.

Hard limits ensure tension:

- max 5 ships
- max 4 deployment kits
- command points remain capped
- target awareness increases

Victory remains about planning.

---

# Summary

Progression in **Starline Pursuit** is built around:

- unlocking new tools
- specializing ships into roles
- increasing tactical options
- scaling difficulty through smarter targets and tighter constraints

It supports a full campaign while preserving the core chase fantasy:

**Evidence → Prediction → Trap → Interception**
