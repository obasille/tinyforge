Design for each **target ship type**, focusing on:

- Narrative role
- Primary objective
- Secondary behavior
- Movement logic
- Reaction to player pressure
- Win / fail condition interaction

All assume movement along star lanes, one jump per turn unless specified.

---

# 1. Smuggler Runner

### Role

Baseline evasive target. Pure pursuit gameplay.

### Primary Objective

Survive for X turns **or** reach a designated exit system on the rim.

### Movement Behavior

- Moves every turn.
- Prefers:
  - Low sensor visibility systems
  - Systems with fewer player ships nearby

- Avoids:
  - Recently scanned systems
  - Systems with active beacons

Decision heuristic:

- Score = (low player proximity) + (low sensor coverage) + (progress toward exit)

### Reaction to Player Pressure

If revealed:

- Immediately chooses path that maximizes distance from nearest interceptor.
- May reverse direction if forward path becomes dangerous.

### Special Trait

- Leaves normal trail decay.

### Gameplay Effect

Encourages wide sensor coverage and predictive blocking.

---

# 2. Pirate Raider

### Role

Unpredictable semi-aggressive target.

### Primary Objective

Accumulate “raid points” by visiting trade hubs before escaping.

### Movement Behavior

- 70% chance: move toward high-value systems.
- 30% chance: loiter 1 extra turn to “raid”.
- Avoids heavy player presence, but less cautious than smuggler.

### Reaction to Player Pressure

If player ships are adjacent:

- Moves aggressively away.
- May sacrifice optimal route to avoid interception.

### Special Trait

- When loitering, emits stronger detectable signature.
- Trail decay slower near raided systems.

### Gameplay Effect

Creates tension: player must decide whether to intercept early or gather intel.

---

# 3. Ghost Probe

### Role

Stealth-heavy, sensor counterplay target.

### Primary Objective

Reach a nebula core or specific stealth destination.

### Movement Behavior

- Prefers:
  - Nebula systems
  - Low-traffic routes

- Avoids:
  - High-visibility hubs

### Special Rules

- Inside nebula:
  - Cannot be detected by passive sensors.
  - Trail decays 1 turn faster.

- Outside nebula:
  - Standard detection.

### Reaction to Player Pressure

If revealed:

- Attempts to re-enter nebula zone.
- Will take longer but safer routes.

### Gameplay Effect

Forces layered sensor strategy and careful nebula monitoring.

---

# 4. Diplomatic Courier

### Role

High-speed, objective-driven runner.

### Primary Objective

Reach one of several diplomatic endpoints.

### Movement Behavior

- Direct route preference.
- Optimizes shortest path distance to target.

### Special Trait

- 20% chance per turn to perform double jump (skip intermediate system).
- Leaves reduced trail intensity.

### Reaction to Player Pressure

If revealed and threatened:

- May use double jump immediately (if available).
- Otherwise maintains course unless direct interception likely.

### Gameplay Effect

Creates urgency. Requires forward interception rather than pure tracking.

---

# 5. Decoy Master

### Role

Misinformation-based target.

### Primary Objective

Survive for X turns.

### Movement Behavior

- Moves cautiously like Smuggler.
- Prefers mid-density routes.

### Special Trait

Every 3–4 turns:

- Drops false trail in adjacent system.
- Fake trail lasts 2 turns.

Sometimes:

- Generates fake sensor ping in nearby system.

### Reaction to Player Pressure

If heavily tracked:

- Intentionally doubles back to create confusion.

### Gameplay Effect

Tests player deduction and resource discipline.
Punishes overreaction to incomplete intel.

---

# 6. Rebel Commander (Advanced Target)

### Role

Strategic, adaptive opponent.

### Primary Objective

Reach allied cluster OR survive for X turns.

### Movement Behavior

- Evaluates:
  - Player ship distribution
  - Sensor coverage density
  - Blocked lanes

- Avoids chokepoints unless safe.

### Special Trait

- Occasionally delays movement to let player overshoot.
- May reroute long-term instead of reacting locally.

### Reaction to Player Pressure

- If one corridor heavily monitored:
  - Switches to secondary corridor early.

- If boxed in:
  - Takes highest-risk path.

### Gameplay Effect

Feels intelligent and reactive.
Strong mind-game element.

---

# 7. Sleeper Agent (Hidden Behavior Target)

### Role

Delayed reveal twist.

### Primary Objective

Reach specific hidden system (unknown to player).

### Movement Behavior

- Appears random for first N turns.
- After condition met:
  - Switches to direct movement mode.

### Special Trait

- Trail initially weak.
- After activation, trail becomes strong but shorter-lived.

### Reaction to Player Pressure

Minimal early reaction.
Late-game behaves like Courier.

### Gameplay Effect

Mid-game twist.
Requires continuous monitoring, not tunnel vision.

| Ghost Probe     | 1     | Medium         | High    | No        | High            |

# Design Impact on Gameplay

Different targets shift optimal player behavior:

- Smuggler → broad sensor nets
- Pirate → timing decisions
- Ghost → nebula control
- Courier → forward interception
- Decoy → disciplined resource usage
- Rebel → predictive strategy
- Sleeper → long-term monitoring
