Design for each **target ship type**, including:

- Narrative role
- Primary and secondary objectives
- Exact win condition type
- Movement behavior
- Reaction to player pressure
- Strategic impact on the player

All targets move along star lanes unless stated otherwise.

---

# 1. Smuggler Runner

### Role

Baseline evasive target focused on pursuit and interception.

### Victory Type

**Escape OR Survival (variant-based)**

- Standard mode: reach one of several rim exit stars.
- Variant mode: survive X turns.

### Primary Objective

Avoid interception while progressing toward escape vector.

### Movement Behavior

- Moves every turn.
- Scores adjacent systems by:
  - Distance from nearest player ship
  - Low sensor coverage
  - Progress toward exit (if escape mode)

- Avoids recently scanned systems.

### Reaction to Player Pressure

- If revealed, immediately increases distance from nearest interceptor.
- May reverse course if forward route is threatened.

### Strategic Impact

Encourages wide sensor placement and predictive blocking without making exits trivial (because survival variant prevents pure chokepoint play).

# 2. Pirate Raider

### Role

Semi-aggressive, opportunistic target.

### Victory Type

**Objective Accumulation (+ optional escape)**

- Win by raiding N trade hubs.
- Optional advanced mode: raid N hubs then escape.

### Primary Objective

Visit high-value hub systems and remain there for 1 turn to raid.

### Movement Behavior

- 70%: move toward nearest high-value hub.
- 30%: linger one turn when raiding.
- Avoids heavy player presence but tolerates moderate risk.

### Reaction to Player Pressure

- If threatened mid-raid, aborts and retreats.
- Will reroute toward alternate hub.

### Strategic Impact

Forces the player to decide:

- Defend hubs
- Intercept early
- Or track and capture after accumulation

Prevents simple exit blocking from solving the map.

# 3. Ghost Probe

### Role

Stealth-focused infiltration target.

### Victory Type

**Zone-Based**

- Reach any nebula core system.

### Primary Objective

Enter nebula region and reach deep core system.

### Movement Behavior

- Prefers routes through nebula lanes.
- Avoids hubs and high-traffic areas.

### Special Mechanics

- Inside nebula:
  - Cannot be detected by passive sensors.
  - Trail decays faster.

- Outside nebula:
  - Standard detectability.

### Reaction to Player Pressure

- If revealed outside nebula, prioritizes re-entry.
- Accepts longer but safer routes.

### Strategic Impact

Forces layered sensor strategy and nebula control.
Blocking exits is irrelevant.

---

# 4. Diplomatic Courier

### Role

High-speed mission runner.

### Victory Type

**Escape (Multiple Valid Destinations)**

- Reach one of several diplomatic endpoint systems.

### Primary Objective

Reach nearest valid endpoint as fast as possible.

### Movement Behavior

- Favors shortest path.
- 20% chance per turn to double-jump (skip one node).

### Special Mechanics

- Reduced trail intensity.
- Rare burst movement.

### Reaction to Player Pressure

- If interception imminent:
  - May trigger double jump.

- Otherwise maintains optimal route.

### Strategic Impact

Encourages forward interception rather than map-wide blocking.
Speed introduces urgency.

---

# 5. Decoy Master

### Role

Deception-based evasive target.

### Victory Type

**Time-Based Survival**

- Survive X turns.

### Primary Objective

Remain uncaptured while confusing tracking efforts.

### Movement Behavior

- Avoids high sensor density.
- Favors mid-density regions to blend.

### Special Mechanics

- Every few turns:
  - Generates false trail in adjacent system.

- Occasionally creates fake sensor pings.

### Reaction to Player Pressure

- Doubles back intentionally.
- Enters loops to exploit over-commitment.

### Strategic Impact

Punishes overreaction.
Forces disciplined investigation rather than brute blocking.

---

# 6. Rebel Commander

### Role

Strategic adaptive opponent.

### Victory Type

**Zone-Based OR Survival**

- Reach allied cluster.
- OR survive X turns.

### Primary Objective

Reach allied territory through safest corridor.

### Movement Behavior

- Evaluates:
  - Player ship density
  - Sensor coverage
  - Chokepoints

- Avoids predictable routes.

### Special Mechanics

- May pause to mislead.
- May reroute long-term rather than react locally.

### Reaction to Player Pressure

- Switches corridors early if one becomes heavily monitored.
- Accepts calculated risks when cornered.

### Strategic Impact

Feels intelligent.
Counters static blocking strategies.

---

# 7. Sleeper Agent

### Role

Delayed-behavior twist target.

### Victory Type

**Hidden Destination**

- Reach one specific hidden system.
- Destination unknown to player until mid-game.

### Primary Objective

Move semi-randomly until activation event.
After activation:

- Direct movement toward hidden goal.

### Special Mechanics

- Early trail weak and misleading.
- Post-activation trail stronger but shorter-lived.

### Reaction to Player Pressure

- Early: minimal reaction.
- Late: behaves like high-efficiency courier.

### Strategic Impact

Prevents early over-optimization.
Requires continuous monitoring rather than tunnel vision.

# Summary of Victory Types

| Target   | Victory Type                   | Blocking Viable? |
| -------- | ------------------------------ | ---------------- |
| Smuggler | Escape or Survival             | Partial          |
| Pirate   | Raid count (+ escape optional) | No               |
| Ghost    | Reach nebula zone              | No               |
| Courier  | Multi-endpoint escape          | Limited          |
| Decoy    | Survival                       | No               |
| Rebel    | Allied zone or survival        | Partial          |
| Sleeper  | Hidden destination             | Unreliable       |

---

# Design Outcome

Not all targets win by reaching exit stars.

Victory types include:

- Escape-based
- Zone-based
- Survival-based
- Objective accumulation
- Hidden destination

This ensures:

- Exit blocking is never universally dominant.
- Sensor placement and inference remain central.
- Resource decisions matter.
- Each target type changes optimal player behavior while preserving core mechanics:
  scan → investigate → deploy → intercept.
